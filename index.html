4 · Rules that make it work

  • Before every run: 500 ml of electrolytes, and a banana or toast an hour before. Carey: no pouch for 3 hours before.
  • Easy means easy: if you can't talk in full sentences, slow down or walk.
  • Heavy legs at 1 km? Slow to a shuffle for 10 minutes instead of stopping.
  • Carey's left shoulder: no dips, pull-ups, overhead pressing or pullovers until it's pain-free. Lower back: no loaded lunges or heavy deadlifts.
  • Miss a session? Move on. Don't double up. Missed a long run? Do it Sunday.
  • Food: high protein every day; rice or potatoes after training and the night before long runs.
  • Weeks 8–9: lighter gym, no leg day in race week.

5 · Race day, Sun Dec 20

  • Fri Dec 18: pick up bibs; lay out shoes, kit, gels (2 each + 1 spare).
  • Sat Dec 19: extra rice or bread, dinner by 7 PM, lights out 9:30.
  • 4:30 wake, 500 ml electrolytes. 4:45 race breakfast (the same one you rehearsed Nov 21 and Dec 5).
  • 5:30 arrive, bathroom, 5 min easy jog.
  • 6:00 start slower than feels right for the first 3K. Gel at km 6 and 12. Water at every station.
  • Km 15–21: stick together and walk the steep bits fast. Finish together.
