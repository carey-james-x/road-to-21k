House rules

Six things that make it work

🌅

Run after sunset

Weekday runs at 7:30–8 PM. Only the two dress rehearsals are at 6 AM, to practise the race clock.

💧

Salt, not just water

500 ml with electrolytes before every run over 45 min. Weigh yourselves before and after one long run to learn your sweat loss.

⚡

Practise fuel

A gel (or 3 dates) at km 6 and km 12 on every long run from 12K up. Nothing new on race day.

👟

Trail-ready shoes

Cross-country course: do the down-week runs on dirt and sand. Decide shoes by Nov 21 and race in what you rehearsed in.

🔁

Miss one, move on

Skipped a Tuesday? Don't double up. Missed a long run? Do it Sunday, or repeat last week's distance.

🫀

Watch the HRV trend

If the 7-day HRV is low and resting HR is 5+ above normal, make the quality day easy. Long run stays.
